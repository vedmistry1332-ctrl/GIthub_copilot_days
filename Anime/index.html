Kokushibō

Upper Moon One

Once a human swordsman, Kokushibō became a demon to surpass his limits. He wields Moon Breathing techniques, has enhanced regeneration, and unmatched combat skill.

Dōma

Upper Moon Two

Dōma is a charismatic yet terrifying demon who uses Ice techniques. He is known for his deceptive charm, cruelty, and mastery of cryokinetic powers in battle.

Akaza

Upper Moon Three

Akaza is a martial artist demon who specializes in destructive hand-to-hand combat. His Blood Demon Art enhances his fighting style with shockwaves and immense strength.
